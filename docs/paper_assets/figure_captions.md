# Figure Captions

- **Fig. 1 (system_architecture):** End-to-end bimodal architecture showing text and face emotion streams, weighted fusion, and response generation.
- **Fig. 2 (runtime_workflow):** Runtime execution flow from input capture through inference, fusion, response generation, and evaluation logging.
- **Fig. 3 (human_eval_workflow):** Human evaluation pipeline from rating-sheet generation to aggregation and report-ready summaries.
- **Fig. 4 (fusion_equation):** Weighted fusion rule and argmax decision used to select final emotion.
- **Fig. 5 (ablation_comparison_plot):** Distribution of fused top emotions from the ablation output.
- **Fig. 6 (alpha_sensitivity_plot):** Fused emotion distribution as alpha varies from 0.0 to 1.0.
- **Fig. 7 (human_eval_summary_plot):** Pilot human-evaluation means for empathy, social presence, trust, and helpfulness.
- **Fig. 8 (helpfulness_plot):** Pilot helpfulness comparison across text-only, face-only, and fused modes.
- **Fig. 9 (case_type_distribution_plot):** Count of congruent, dissonant, and ambiguous cases in the evaluation set.
