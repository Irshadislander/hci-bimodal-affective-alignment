# Presentation Assets

## Slide: Architecture (One-Slide Script)
- **Title:** Bimodal Affective Alignment Architecture
- **Visual:** `figures/system_architecture.png`
- **Talk track:** The system processes user text and facial image cues in parallel, estimates emotion distributions per modality, fuses them with weighted alpha logic, and generates an empathetic response from the fused emotion.

## Slide: Evaluation (One-Slide Script)
- **Title:** Evaluation Design and Workflow
- **Visuals:** `figures/runtime_workflow.png` + `figures/human_eval_workflow.png`
- **Talk track:** Automatic experiments produce ablation and alpha-sensitivity artifacts, while a structured human-rating workflow captures empathy, social presence, trust, and helpfulness.

## Slide: Key Findings (One-Slide Script)
- **Title:** Key Findings (Classroom Prototype)
- **Visuals:** `figures/alpha_sensitivity_plot.png` + `figures/human_eval_summary_plot.png`
- **Talk track:** Fusion behavior changes with alpha as expected, and preliminary pilot ratings (R1) favor fused mode over text-only and face-only in all four human metrics.

## Slide: Limitations and Future Work (One-Slide Script)
